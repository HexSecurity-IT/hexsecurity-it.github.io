Website privacy

Page views

Our report counts page views and service openings by day. These totals contain the page or service name and the count. A page view is counted again if you reload the page. The site skips these counts when your browser sends Global Privacy Control or enables Do Not Track.

Enquiries

When you send an enquiry to Hex Security, we save the name, mobile number, city and service you submit, along with the time received, in our private Cloudflare database and Google Sheets report. Our team uses these details to reply. We receive them when you press a send button.

The website enquiry can include a reply email address and a message. These optional details are queued for delivery to our business inbox. They are not copied into the Google Sheets report. If you choose WhatsApp, review the draft in that app before sending it.

Website assistant

The assistant handles typed questions on the page. Hex Security does not save the chat or record microphone audio. If you use the Speak button, your browser may process speech using its online recognition service. You can type instead.
